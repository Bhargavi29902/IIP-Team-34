About FollowUp AI

FollowUp AI turns scattered audit commitments into a trackable workflow. AI proposes, a human decides, and every step leaves evidence.

What the AI does

  • Reads pasted text and suggests tasks, owners and dates, each with the exact source sentence.
  • Drafts follow-up emails, marked as AI-written.
  • Masks emails, phone numbers and NRIC numbers before text reaches the AI service.

What it does not do

  • It never sends an email or creates a calendar event without a person approving it.
  • It never guesses a missing or vague date. Those are flagged “Needs confirmation”.
  • It can be wrong. Always check the source evidence.
  • It is a prototype that runs on fictional demo data, not real client data.
  • Instructions hidden inside an email are treated as data, never as commands.
